RupayVibe - Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: November 2026 | Contact: rupayvibe.official@gmail.com

1. Privacy Policy

RupayVibe collects basic account details (Name, Email, User ID) via Google Sign-In solely to track earned coins, prevent fraud, and process user reward redemptions. We integrate third-party survey and ad providers (such as TheoremReach and Google AdMob) which process anonymous device/user identifiers to deliver relevant surveys and rewards. We never sell personal user data.

2. Terms of Service

Users must provide honest and accurate responses in surveys. Automated bots, VPNs, multiple accounts, or fraudulent activity will result in immediate account suspension and forfeiture of rewards. Coins earned can be redeemed once the minimum threshold is reached.
