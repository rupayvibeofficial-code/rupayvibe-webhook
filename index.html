About RupayVibe

RupayVibe is an online consumer rewards and market research engagement platform designed for mobile users in India. We connect everyday users with verified global market research partners so brands can gather genuine consumer feedback while users earn reward coins for their valuable time.

How It Works

  • 1. Create a Free Profile: Users register inside the RupayVibe Android application using Google Sign-In.
  • 2. Complete Surveys & Activities: Browse available partner surveys, share honest responses, and earn instant reward coins upon successful completion.
  • 3. Redeem Rewards: Once the minimum redemption threshold is reached, users can request payouts directly via supported digital payment methods (UPI).

Privacy Policy & Data Consent

Effective Date: November 2026

Your privacy is important to us. RupayVibe collects only the minimum information required to operate the rewards wallet and match users with relevant surveys:

  • Information Collected: Name, email address, unique user identifier (UID), device advertising ID, and basic demographic details provided voluntarily for survey matching.
  • Third-Party Survey & Ad Partners: We partner with reputable market research providers (such as TheoremReach) and advertising networks (Google AdMob). When you participate in a survey, anonymized demographic and session identifiers are shared strictly to qualify and credit your survey rewards.
  • User Consent & Control: Participation in surveys is completely voluntary. Users may stop using the platform or request deletion of their account data at any time by contacting our support team.
  • Data Security: All user balances and records are secured using encrypted cloud infrastructure (Google Firebase). We never sell personal contact details to unauthorized third parties.

GDPR & CCPA Data Protection Rights

GDPR (European Economic Area Residents): Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), users in the EEA have the right to access, update, rectify, or request deletion of their personal data, restrict or object to data processing, request data portability, and withdraw consent at any time.

CCPA (California Residents): Under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), California residents have the right to know what personal data is collected, request deletion of their personal information, and opt out of the sale of personal data. RupayVibe does not sell user personal data.

To exercise any of your GDPR or CCPA privacy rights, please contact us at rupayvibe.official@gmail.com.
